Programs offered

Three paths to your license

Our barber education program prepares students for their licensing exam while focusing on professionalism, creativity, and business acumen.

01

Barber Program

1250 Hours

10 Months+ · PA Barber License. A blend of practical and theoretical training. Students will be prepared to pass the PA State Board of Barber Licensing Examination.

Request info →

02

Barber Teacher’s Program

500 Hours

10 Months+ · PA Barber Instructor License. Designed to educate licensed barber managers on how to instruct others on the theory and art of barbering.

Request info →

03

Crossover Program

PA Barber

PA Barber License for PA Cosmetology license holders. For those who have already obtained a PA Cosmetology license and would like to obtain their PA Barber license as well.

Our school

2,800 sq/ft in the heart of downtown

The Cutting Edge Barber Academy is a 2,800 sq/ft multifaceted education center. Our clinic floor and practical learning area features 10 barber stations, 6 wash stations, and all other necessary equipment needed for hands-on training.

With over 20 years of experience in the barber industry, our instructors are highly skilled, knowledgeable, and use the latest teaching methods.

  • Traditional barber shop techniques with modern barbering style and flair

Careers in barbering

More than just a license

After graduating and obtaining your barbering license, there’s an array of job opportunities to explore in the hair industry:

  • Barber

  • Barber Owner
