FAQs

Questions we often hear

What conditions can chiropractic care help with?

Chiropractic care is effective for back pain, neck pain, headaches, joint pain, sciatica, and more, helping to restore mobility and alleviate discomfort.

Is chiropractic care safe?

Yes, chiropractic care is a safe, non-invasive treatment for most patients. Our professionals use gentle techniques tailored to each patient’s needs.

How many chiropractic sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies based on the individual and the condition. After an initial evaluation, we’ll recommend a treatment plan that suits you.

Can I receive both chiropractic and acupuncture treatments?

Yes. Ask us at your visit, or call (251) 344-8588.
