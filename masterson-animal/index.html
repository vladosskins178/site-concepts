What we do

Every aspect of having a pet

Our services include preventative care, surgery, dental, emergency treatment, trauma, rehabilitation, in-house diagnostics, and boarding.

Medicine

Preventive care, flea and tick treatments, heart-worm prevention, dental care and pet micro chipping, with full spectrum in-house diagnostics.

Surgery

Surgery, including spaying & neutering, from a team of experienced veterinarians focused on the finest care of your pet.

Boarding

Going on a vacation? Relax knowing your pet will be in a climate controlled dorm. When you pick up your friend, he’ll be clean and smelling great.

Our doctors

Experienced vets who know your pet

Dr. Jeff McGregor founded Masterson Animal Clinic in 1997, after practicing small animal surgery and medicine at St Matthews Animal Clinic in Louisville. He enjoys preventive care, dentistry, general medicine, critical care, geriatric care, hospice care, anesthesia and especially surgery.

Dr. Jennifer Bridgham spent almost 6 years at AA Small Animal Emergency Service before her daytime practice, where she enjoys getting to know her patients almost as well as her own pets.

  • Both graduated from the University Of Wisconsin- Madison School Of Veterinary Medicine
  • Over 25 years combined experience

Before your visit

Good to know

How do I set up an appointment?

If you have a question for the doctor or you would like to set up an appointment, please give us a call at 859-389-8387.

Are there forms to fill in ahead of time?

Yes. Boarding agreements, consent forms, dental consent and the patient information record can be downloaded from our patient forms page.

How do I find the clinic?

We are located at 1490 Leestown Rd. Lexington, KY. MAC is ¾ mile inside New Circle. If you are coming from downtown Lexington, we are about ½ mile past Forbes Rd.
