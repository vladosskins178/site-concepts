Before your first class

Good to know

I’ve never done yoga. Where do I start?

Our Beginners class is the perfect way to start: gentle, introductory, suitable for all ages, fitness levels and body types. No experience is necessary.

How do I sign up for a class?

Open the class schedule or download the Mindbody App to save time.

What if I can’t make it?

Please use our app to un-enroll 4 hours prior to a class so others have the opportunity to attend. New Student Special no-shows and late cancels have a $10 fee.

Can I come late to aerial, lyra or bungee?

Late arrivals will not be permitted into these classes, for the safety of students and teachers.

What happens in bad weather?

We take our lead from the local school districts with snow cancellations, and post any closings on our schedule and on Facebook.
