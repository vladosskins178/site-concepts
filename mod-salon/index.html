What our guests say

Good to know

Before your visit

How do I choose the right stylist for my hair needs?

We have four stylist levels, from Associate to Master, allowing you to select a stylist that fits your budget and desired expertise. Our team is happy to help guide you to the perfect match.

Do you offer modern hair colour techniques like ombre?

Yes, our stylists specialize in modern colouring techniques including balayage, ombre, highlights, and creative colour.

Can I book a consultation before committing to a service?

Absolutely. We offer complimentary consultations to discuss your hair goals before you commit to any service with us.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Changes between 24 and 48 hours before the appointment are subject to a $50 fee. Changes made less than 24 hours before, including no-shows, may be charged up to 50% of the estimated service cost.

Are you open Mondays?

Yes. We welcome guests on Mondays, 10:00am – 5:00pm. We are open Monday to Saturday and closed Sunday.
