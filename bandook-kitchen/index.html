Our story

From Bristol’s Wapping Wharf to Milsom Place

Our story began in Bristol, where the team behind the award-winning fine dining Indian restaurants Mint Room in Bath and Bristol had a vision to create a modern, casual dining, Indian street food restaurant.

Bandook, a term which in the Urdu/Hindi language means “gun” or “rifle”, proved to be an instant hit with both the public and critics alike. Located in Milsom Place, just off the fashionable Milsom Street, Bandook Bath boasts an eye-catching interior inspired by the colonial era.
