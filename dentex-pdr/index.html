Frequently asked questions

FAQs answered

What is paintless dent removal (PDR)?

Paintless dent removal is an innovative process for repairing minor dents, dings and hail damage with specialized tools and without using paint, replacement panels or body filler.

How long will the job take?

Dentex offers same-day repairs on most jobs. In fact, paintless dent removal can usually be done in 1 to 3 hours.

What size dents do you remove?

Most dents are the size of your palm or smaller. Many are approximately the size of a coin. Dentex can repair large dents, including ones the size of a basketball (or larger).

What should I do if my paint is scratched or chipped?

We can still remove dents if there is a scratch or chipped paint. We can remove minor scratches but cannot remove deep scratches. Paint chips can be touched up after the repair is done as desired.

Will the dent reappear?

No. Once PDR removes the dent, it won’t come back. This is your Dentex guarantee.

How much will my dent removal cost?

The cost depends on the dent's size and depth. We don’t charge per dent but instead on the overall job. To give an accurate estimate, we need to ask you a few questions, so please give us a call.
