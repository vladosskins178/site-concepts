Banquets

Share a feast

From the seemingly infinite diversity of culinary creations of the Indian subcontinent, we have chosen some of the more characteristic, yet legendary dishes.

Singh’s Special Banquet

2 people only. Vegetable samosa, then butter chicken, rogan josh, rice, naan and pappadums.

Maharaja Banquet

3 or more people, eat as much as you like. Seekh kebab, chicken tikka and samosa; butter chicken, rogan josh, beef vindaloo and mixed vegetables; rice, mixed naan and dessert of the night.

Seafood Banquet

3 or more people, eat as much as you like. Tandoori prawns, chicken tikka and samosa; butter chicken, rogan josh, fish Malabar and mango prawns; rice, mixed breads and dessert of the night.

About us

The clay oven, since 1998

Our most experienced chefs offer you the delights of India’s cuisine, specialising in Indian clay oven and sizzler dishes. The traditional tandoor is renowned for the mysterious and unique flavour it gives to meat and breads.

Established in 1998 in Albury, with our Wodonga restaurant opened in 2007. We do not compromise quality for price.

  • Fully qualified chefs from India
  • Traditional North Indian & Goan cuisines
  • Fully licensed: local and imported beers and wines
  • Fully air conditioned, candles on the tables
