Good to know

Before you book

Why do prices say “from”?

Our services are based on a level pricing system depending upon the individual stylist. Levels reflect experience, education and client demand.

How do bridal party reservations work?

18% gratuity is added to all bridal party reservations of 4 or more, and we require a credit card number. Cancellations within one week of the reservation date result in a charge of 50% of the total estimated service fee.

Do you offer packages or parties?

Yes: individual packages from $151 and group packages from $86 per person. Please call 515-280-5358 to inquire.

Do you work on-site for shoots and events?

Yes: television appearances, video and photography shoots, fashion shows, education events and bespoke projects. Call 515-280-5358 to discuss plans and pricing.
