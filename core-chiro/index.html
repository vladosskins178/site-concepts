New patients

Your first visit

What does the $25 new patient visit include?

X-rays & Consultation at our Evansville, IN office. Excludes Medicare, Medicaid, & Core Health Management. Book online or call 812-777-4004.

What happens at the first visit?

Check in with the front desk for a brief tour of our office, discuss your wellness goals with our team, then meet with the Doctor for a consultation and, if clinically indicated, X-rays.

Do I need to fill out paperwork?

Please either complete your paperwork in advance or arrive 10 minutes early to complete it. Forms for adults and for ages 0-2, 2-12 and 13-18 are on our New Patients page.

When is my first adjustment?

A second visit is scheduled to review your findings with the Doctor, complete your first adjustment, and discuss recommended care. For pediatric and pregnant patients, the first adjustment is done during the initial visit.

When are appointments available?

Monday-Thursday from 9 AM-1 PM and 3-6 PM. Your appointment is not confirmed until you receive a call from our team.
