Our bridal process

From first hello to “I do”

Our step-by-step bridal booking process ensures the seamless flow of events on your special day.

01

Initial Consultation

A free consultation to discuss your hair and makeup needs and event day details: everything from the style of your dress to scheduling and logistics.

Not a trial run, and it does not secure your event date and time.
02

Service Trial Run

We create your custom hair and makeup design. Most clients use this opportunity to have their bridal portraits taken. Feel free to bring a friend!

A 50% deposit on your total services package secures your date and time.
03

Event Day

Our team arrives at your destination at our agreed scheduled timing, with all materials needed to execute your customized design.

Enjoy & relax, we’ll take it from here!

Good to know

Bridal questions

Is the consultation free?

Yes. Southern Couture offers a free consultation to answer any questions you may have. Hair styling and makeup are not applied during the consult.

How do I secure my wedding date?

A 50% deposit on your total services package is required to secure your date and time. Booking a consultation alone does not secure it.

Is a trial required?

A trial is not required but highly recommended. Most of our clients use it to have their bridal portraits taken.

Can my bridal party get ready at the salon?

Yes. Book our private Bridal Bar suite for you and your party. Email us to inquire.
