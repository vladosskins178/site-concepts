Our story

From a food cart to Sherman Avenue

Banzo was born from a late-night conversation between Netalee and Aaron, who, after tiring of New York City’s grind, dreamed of bringing authentic Mediterranean falafel to Madison, WI. They hit the road from New York with a food cart hitched and hopes high.

The fresh, made-from-scratch falafel and hummus quickly won over students and locals alike. Today, Banzo’s mission remains the same: to serve up delicious and nourishing food made with care.
