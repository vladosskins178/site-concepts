Emergency services

A true dental emergency is seen immediately

True dental emergencies, once identified, will be prioritized to be seen immediately at our clinic.

Tooth pain

Unrelieved by over-the-counter pain medications at their maximum dosage.

Bleeding

That is continuous without any signs of slowing down.

Swelling

That has developed quickly and hinders movement and function.

Trauma

To the teeth and surrounding structures, with one or more of the above.

Call 902-444-1948. If we are closed or you are unable to contact our office and are presenting with any of the above, please head to the nearest ER or call 911.

3D digital scanning

Our office is Primescan enabled

The innovative Primescan digital impression technology scans over 1,000,000 3D points per second, producing incredibly photo realistic and highly accurate data.

We use these scans with digital design software to customize your treatment with CAD/CAM 3D printing and milling technologies in collaboration with local dental laboratories.

  • Digital Radiography: Precision, Safety, Speed, Comfort
  • Proudly using Sidexis 4 Digital Imaging
  • Veneer shape and colour approved on screen before they are made

Sedation dentistry

Dental fear and anxiety are very common

We will ask you a few questions on the intake form to get an idea of your comfort level before we even seat you in the dental chair.

We currently offer mild oral sedation at our clinic, and refer cases that need gas sedation, IV sedation or general anesthesia to other clinics and the VG hospital.

Good to know

Before your visit

How do I book an appointment?

Call 902-444-1948 or send an appointment request on our contact page. Complete the form for each person needing an appointment.

Do you see children?

Yes. Children that are 14 years of age or younger and have a valid Nova Scotia MSI Health Card have basic dental coverage from the provincial government.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Tooth pain unrelieved by maximum-dose over-the-counter medication, continuous bleeding, quickly developing swelling, or trauma with any of these. Lost fillings and chips without pain, bleeding or swelling will be prioritized for treatment, though they are not a true emergency.

Can I get clear aligners here?

In less complex cases, Dr. Awad will be able to offer you clear aligner treatment at our clinic using SureSmile. Many times we will refer to an orthodontist.

When are you open?

Monday to Thursday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Friday, Saturday and Sunday closed.
