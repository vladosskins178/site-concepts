How to order

Pre-order online, or just call

Order online, from a select menu, with 3 days notice. Our current online menu includes croissants, pies, baguettes, scali, specialty and custom cakes.

Holiday menus for Valentine’s, Pi Day, Easter, Thanksgiving and Christmas usually go up a couple weeks in advance, all on the same online menu.

  • Same-day hold: call (608) 257-1652
  • Future orders also in person or by phone: we confirm with the bakers, then call you back for payment
  • Custom cakes, including writing, with 3 days notice

Established 2009 on Willy Street

A local institution

Batch opened September 2nd, 2009, in a converted Trachte building at 1511 Williamson St. We found our groove there, but quickly outgrew the space, and moved one block to our current digs at 1402 Willy in 2013.

With your help, Batch has become a local institution and we love continuing the rich baking history of Madison.

Good to know

Before you stop in

Can I hold something from today’s case?

Yes. Call us at (608) 257-1652 to see what’s in the case or place a same-day hold.

How much notice do online orders need?

3 days. Order online from a select menu: croissants, pies, baguettes, scali, specialty and custom cakes.

Where else can I buy your bread?

Our breads are available exclusively on Willy Street: at our retail shop and the Willy St Coop, 2 blocks away.

Are you hiring?

We are always accepting resumes from experienced bakers. Send your resume and a brief statement to retail@batchbakehouse.com.
