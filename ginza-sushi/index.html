Our story

Enjoyable, exciting, and a form of art

Here at Ginza, we believe that food should be more than just something that you eat: It should be enjoyable, exciting, and a form of art. Our goal is to create a unique culinary experience in which our dishes are made with the purest, freshest, and the finest ingredients to deliver the best food possible.

Founded in December of 2018 by Mr. Chen, his passion for cooking and food has led him to open four restaurants over the past 25 years. With this experience and expertise, he has been able to perfect and refine the art of Asian cuisine.
