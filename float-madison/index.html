Your first float

Nothing to bring but yourself

Each of our float suites has a private dressing area and shower. Shampoo, conditioner, bodywash, earplugs and towels are provided.

FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to bring? Swim suit?

Nothing but yourself and an excited and open mind. We provide eco-friendly shampoo, conditioner and body wash, plus towels, robes, earplugs, q-tips and hair dryers. Since our float tanks, changing areas and showers are each in their own private rooms there’s no need to wear anything, but you are more than welcome to bring along a swim suit.

What should I do to prepare?

Avoid shaving or waxing for at least 24 hours prior to your float. Don’t use stimulants such as caffeine for several hours beforehand. Eat a meal about 60-90 minutes prior to your appointment.

I dyed my hair recently. Can I float?

Do not float if you’ve dyed your hair in the past 10 days. With red/pink dye, we require waiting 30+ days to float. You cannot float if you have Henna coloring in your hair (or a Henna tattoo). Full hair dye policy

Can I float longer than 60 minutes?

Yes. Book two sessions back-to-back for a 2+ hour float and get up to an extra 45 minutes free, or call to schedule a 90 minute float.

How far ahead can I book?

We service by scheduled appointment only and often book out a day or more in advance. You can view our schedule and book online up to 60 days in advance.
