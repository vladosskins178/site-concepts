Good to know

Questions, answered

What are the Little Rock hours?

Andy’s #9 on W Markham St is open Mon–Fri 6am–7pm and Sat 6am–3pm, closed Sunday. Closing time may vary due to staffing — call (501) 224-2444 to check.

Which locations are open on Sunday?

Camden (6am–7pm), Magnolia (6am–2pm), Crossett (6am–9pm), Arkadelphia (6am–9pm) and El Dorado (6am–2pm). Little Rock and Malvern are closed Sundays.

Is the beef really never frozen?

Yes. Andy’s burgers use fresh, never frozen, domestic-only Black Angus ground beef, processed and delivered twice a week by a local supplier.

Are coffee refills free?

Yes — fresh brewed coffee comes with free refills, along with juices and milk at breakfast.

Where can I see menu prices?

Prices are posted on Andy’s menu on Facebook, and specials vary by location — ask at your nearest Andy’s.
