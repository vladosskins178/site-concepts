How we work

All hair, all people

Craft haircuts and color services with low maintenance, natural texture styling.

01

Inclusivity

Services designed to embrace all genders and celebrate every natural hair texture: curly hair education, identity-affirming services and avant-garde haircutting, kept accessible.

02

Sustainability

With Green Circle Salons we recover and repurpose up to 95% of our salon waste, and compost hair clippings on-site. We are proud to be a Climate Neutral Business.

03

Accessibility

ADA accessible and non-gendered restrooms, same level street parking, quiet services, minimal mirror appointments and fragrance-free, allergy conscious product options.

04

Transparent pricing

Set, what-you-see-is-what-you-pay rates that include gratuity. Add-On Extra Time is available at no cost for high density and tightly coiled clients.

05

Clean products

Cruelty-free, largely vegan products in recyclable packaging. All products are sulfate, silicone, paraben, and gluten free. No artificial fragrance or aerosols.

06

Become a hair model

We are always looking for hair models wanting to try something new. Hair modeling is both texture and gender inclusive. Ask us by email.

Pick the right appointment

Pricing is based on length, time, and stylist expertise, never on gender. A non-refundable $50 deposit is required for all new client appointments and is applied to your service total.

New clients

  • 2 Hour HaircutFor most shape shifts.
  • 2.5 Hour HaircutTransformational shape changes, extra long hair, or a deeper dive into styling.
  • 1.5 Hour New Client Short HaircutHair already clippered, cropped, or chin length and above.
  • In-Person Color ConsultationFor first time color clients we require a Good Sister haircut first. We build the house before we paint it!

Established clients

  • 30 Minute Frame + FringeFace frame or bang shape up, for a cut within the last 2-5 months.
  • 1 Hour Maintenance Dry CutOn clean, dry hair, if it has been less than 6-9 months.
  • 1.5 Hour Maintenance Cut with Wash + StyleRecommended at least once a year.
  • 1 Hour Short Hair Maintenance CutHair above the chin, cut every 6-12 weeks.

Our story

Made for a one‑on‑one experience

Co-founders Meg Landis and Alexa Kuper nearly left the hair industry due to the impersonal feel of many traditional salons.

In January of 2021 they launched an intimate, client-focused hair studio. Growing solely by word-of-mouth, Good Sister has scaled into a full salon space in the Northshore.

FAQ

Good to know

How can I book?

Online through Vagaro. Take a moment to look at our team first so you connect with the right stylist. Appointments over $225 require a non-refundable $50 deposit.

How should I prep my hair?

Arrive with clean, dry hair styled in your usual manner that has not been stretched by hair ties, up-styling, or excessive tangling. Your go-to product is totally fine. Come as you are, and we’ll handle the rest.

What if I’m running late or need to cancel?

You get a 15 minute grace period; text your stylist. Cancel through the Vagaro site or app with at least 48 hours notice. Cancellations at or under 24 hours are subject to a 50% fee, and no shows are charged 100%.

Is there extra charge for thick or coily hair?

No. Those with thicker hair types or tightly coiled hair can choose the Extra Time option at no extra charge.
