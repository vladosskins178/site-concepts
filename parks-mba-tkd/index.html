Programs

A class for every age

Regardless of your age, gender, fitness level or ability, our Taekwondo program will help you become stronger and healthier.

Tiny Tigers

4.5 to 5.5 years old

FUN games and exercises for the little ones with early exposure to the basic kicks, punches and forms of Taekwondo, to build confidence, social skills and coordination.

Taekwondo for Kids

6 to 13 years old

All-round physical fitness while learning the fundamentals of Olympic-Style Taekwondo, in a positive, goal-oriented and encouraging environment.

Adult Taekwondo

Age 13+ and 14+ classes

A whole body workout that combines self-discipline, strength building, weight loss and self-defense, within the limitations of your current fitness level.

Good to know

Before your first class

How do I try a class?

Private and trial classes are available by booking. Call (250)477-5419 or email parksmbatkd@gmail.com to reserve your spot.

What ages do you teach?

Tiny Tigers (4.5 to 5.5 years old), Taekwondo for Kids (6 to 13 years old), and adult classes for ages 13+ and 14+.

Is there a discount?

Provincial Government Employees get a 10% discount. Please present your official identification card when you sign up for a regular membership.

Are belt ranks recognized elsewhere?

Yes. All belt ranks achieved at Park’s MBA Taekwondo are recognized worldwide at all World Taekwondo Headquarters (KUKKIWON) & World Taekwondo Federation Schools.

What are the benefits of Taekwondo?

Discipline, a healthy lifestyle, improved focus, heart health, strength and better concentration.
