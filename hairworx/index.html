Questions, answered

Can I book online?

Yes — book any time through our online booking, or call (402) 895-6600.

Are you open on Mondays?

We are open six days a week, Tuesday through Sunday. Tue–Thu 10 am–8 pm, Fri 10 am–7 pm, Sat 9 am–6 pm, Sun 12–6 pm.

How much is a haircut?

Women's haircuts are $25, men's $20 and kids' cuts (12 & under) $17.

Please call us at (402) 895-6600 or email am402hair@outlook.com for pricing on color, chemical services, styling and waxing.
