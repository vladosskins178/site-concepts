Questions

Good to know

Do you do birthday parties?

We LOVE hosting birthday parties! You can book your party by calling our shop (937-963-2366) or by sending us an email. You can even rent the ENTIRE PARK after hours, just ask!

How long will a repair take?

Repair times can vary but we can usually do small repairs like a tube change while you wait. Tune Ups and other services are usually finished and the bike is back to you in a week or less.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment necessary. We are open 7 days a week so drop off your bike whenever it works for your schedule.

What brands do you service?

We service most makes and models.

What should I wear?

Mikes Bike Park can be chilly in the Winter and warm in the Summer. Make sure you wear appropriate clothes for riding at all times.

Where do I park?

We have a small parking lot at the entrance to our building. When that fills up you are welcome to park on the sidewalk in front of our building or on the opposite side of the building (on Crane Street).
