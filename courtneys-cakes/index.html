Good to know

Before you order

Can I order online?

Yes. Choose your cake, size and flavor in our online shop.

Can you make a fully custom design?

Absolutely. Send us your idea and inspiration photos through our custom order form and we’ll get back to you with a quote.

How big are the cakes?

Most celebration cakes start at 6" by 4", which serves 10-12. Larger sizes are available in the shop.

Do you offer cookie classes?

Yes, join Courtney and learn the art of cookie decorating this holiday season. Call 865-200-5935 to ask about dates.
