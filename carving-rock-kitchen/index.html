Our story

Eating out should be enjoyable for ALL

When owner and baker, Lezli-O’Neal Lewis, found out about her wheat allergy eight years ago, she began figuring out how to make gluten-free versions of the comfort foods and treats she missed the most.

After selling her baked goods at markets, she created something that didn’t yet exist in the Chattanooga area: a space where everyone in your party can fully enjoy a meal without worry.

  • Seasonal, local, non-gmo and organic products as much as possible
  • Allergen safe procedures, and happy to chat about any allergens you have

More than a bakery

Community, sustainability, integrity

The values behind every bake.

01

Community

We host a Hope Community Fridge Pantry: leave what you can and take what you need. We source from local and women owned businesses and give retail space to local artisans.

02

Sustainability

We work with New Terra to recycle and compost the majority of our waste, and serve as a refill station for Frequency Fillery. Bring a reusable container for to-go items.

03

Integrity

As a dedicated gluten-free establishment, we make every effort to ensure that every product we use is gluten-free, and we monitor recalls of cross contaminated products.

Good to know

Gluten free, without worry

Is everything really gluten free?

Yes, EVERYTHING on our menu is 100% Gluten free and made in a dedicated gluten free kitchen. We are also dye and soy free and have a variety of dairy-free and vegan options.

What about other allergens?

Dairy, eggs, nuts, sesame, corn and others are present in our kitchen. We cannot guarantee no cross contact, but we are happy to chat with you about our procedures and any allergens you have.

How do I see today’s menu?

Daily options vary. Follow us on Instagram or Facebook for the most updated info on our menu options for the day.

How do I bake my Take and Bake order?

Most items: store in the freezer, thaw overnight in the fridge, bring to about room temperature, then bake. Exact times and temperatures for each item are on our Take and Bake instructions page.
