Our services

Three ways to glow

Professional tanning and skin-enhancing services for beautiful, consistent results.

UV tanning

Non-invasive light therapy designed to improve skin tone, texture and overall radiance, with no recovery time.

Good to know

Before you come in

How do I book?

By phone, email or WhatsApp text: 250-386-4826 or cabanatan2016@gmail.com. Online booking is coming soon.

Can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome on weekdays based on availability; booking ahead reduces wait time. Weekends are first-come, first-served.

Is there a welcome offer?

Buy a Point Package on your first visit and enjoy your first session free.

Do points expire?

All points and/or sessions expire 2 years from the date of purchase. Points and sessions are not transferable or refundable.

Is there an age limit?

UV tanning is not permitted for individuals under 18 years of age.
