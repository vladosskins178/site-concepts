Our story

Family values, since January 1988

Cline’s Heating and Air was started by Jim and Sharon Cline, with their children David and Helen, on one idea: treat customers the way we’d like to be treated, and quality, craftsmanship, honesty and fairness will follow.

Today Cline’s continues under owners Michael and Treasure Boston — proudly veteran and family owned, serving residential and commercial clients across Eastern Washington and Northern Idaho.

FAQs & myths

Straight answers

Don’t see your question? Call 509-443-6146 and get an answer from our team.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. After-hours emergency service is available — call 509-443-6146. Our office is open Monday–Friday, 7:30 am – 4:30 pm.

My furnace or AC is over 10 years old. Does it need replacing?

Not necessarily. We have seen 10-year-old equipment that needs replacing, but it’s rare if it was installed properly and maintained. In our experience it’s not uncommon for equipment to last 30-plus years. We diagnose and repair first.

Which brands do you work on?

We service and repair all brands and install most brands, though we do not service or install oil appliances. We’re the Factory Authorized Carrier Dealer in Spokane County.

Are maintenance plans really necessary?

Yes. Dirty filters or coils, worn belts, low refrigerant or plugged condensate are a recipe for failure — usually when you need the system most. Our Cline’s Club plans check all of these to catch problems before they become catastrophic.

Where do you work?

We serve residential and commercial clients in Spokane, Spokane Valley and across Eastern Washington and Northern Idaho. Our techs hold the federal, state and local licenses required in both Washington and Idaho.

How do I book, and can I finance?

Book online anytime through our scheduler, or call us. Financing is available.
