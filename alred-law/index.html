Straight answers

Questions we hear every day

The short version. In your free consultation we’ll look at your situation specifically.

Do I have a case?

It depends on the circumstances of your incident and the extent of your injuries or damages. A consultation with our legal team can help clarify your position and your potential for a successful claim.

How much does a lawyer cost?

Nothing upfront. We work on a contingency fee basis for personal injury cases — we only receive a fee if we win your case.

How long do I have to file?

In Oklahoma you generally have two years from the date of the accident to file a lawsuit against those responsible for your injuries.

What if I was partly at fault?

Oklahoma follows a modified comparative negligence rule: you can still recover as long as your share of fault is less than 51%. Your compensation is reduced by your percentage of fault.

What damages can I recover?

Economic damages such as medical expenses and lost wages, non-economic damages such as pain and suffering, and in some cases punitive damages for particularly reckless behavior.

How long does a case take?

It varies with the complexity of the case, the damages claimed and the willingness of both sides to settle. Some cases resolve in a few months; others can take several years.
