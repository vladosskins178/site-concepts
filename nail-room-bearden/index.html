Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?

Book online any time at our online booking page, or call the salon at (865) 963-8302.

Can I request a specific nail tech?

Yes. When booking online, click the tech’s name and also type their name in the Note section — that’s how your request reaches us.

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes — 10% off for UT students, nurses and Knox County teachers.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday 10:00 am – 6:00 pm, Saturday 10:00 am – 5:00 pm and Sunday 12:00 pm – 5:00 pm.

Are you the same salon as The Nail Room downtown?

No. The Nail Room Bearden (thenailroombearden.com) and The Nail Room in downtown Knoxville (thenailroomknoxville.com) have been independently owned and operated since December 27, 2021.
