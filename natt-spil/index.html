Inside Nattspil

A tiny room that seats fifty

Almost all of our tables are either for 2 or 4 guests. They all turn over quickly and so there is seldom a long wait.

Groups & parties

The Opium Den for 8 to 12

We do not accept reservations for groups smaller than eight guests. At the back of our restaurant is our “Opium Den”. This room can seat a party of 8-10 comfortably (and up to 12 if you’re really a close friendly group).

We accept reservations for weekday evenings (Sun-Thu) for this room. We are too busy to accept reservations on Fridays and Saturdays.

  • Groups of 25-50 guests: occasionally, Sunday-Wednesday only
  • Cocktail & appetizer parties between 4-7pm
  • Live DJs
