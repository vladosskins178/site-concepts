FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer emergency HVAC services?

Yes. We provide emergency heating and cooling services and often address emergencies within 2-4 hours. Call (423) 802-8837.

How often should my system be serviced?

Homeowners generally benefit from servicing their systems twice per year. Seasonal maintenance helps reduce unexpected breakdowns and improves energy efficiency.

When is repair enough, and when should I replace?

We look at system age, frequency of breakdowns, repair cost and energy efficiency. If the system is newer and the issue is minor, repair is usually the more practical choice. You get clear recommendations without pressure.

Can you improve my home’s efficiency and comfort?

Yes, through equipment upgrades, system evaluations and performance recommendations. We identify airflow issues, outdated components and improper system sizing.
