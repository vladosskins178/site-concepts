Good to know

Booking & cancellations

How much notice do I need to cancel?

We kindly require a minimum of 48 hours' notice for any appointment cancellations or rescheduling requests.

Is a deposit required?

No deposit is required at the time of booking. A valid credit card is required to secure all appointments, whether booked online, over the phone, or in person.

What if something comes up?

We understand life happens. Please contact the salon as soon as possible at (705) 792-2858 so we can assist you.

Are you hiring?

Charisma is growing and currently looking for new team members. Send your resume to charismahs@outlook.com.
