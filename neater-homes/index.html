Why build with us

The Neater Homes difference

Founded by Colin Neate in 1982, Neater Homes has more than four decades of experience designing and building custom homes in Cairns and Far North Queensland.

Our in-house Building Designer and Interior Designer work closely with each client, providing a complete design and construction service from concept to completion.

  • In-house design & construction
  • Personalised service
  • Efficient build times
  • Financially secure, self funded company

Our process

Our home building process in Cairns begins with a conversation.

01

Custom home consultation

We discuss your lifestyle, block of land, ideas, priorities and the budget you would like us to design around.

02

Site assessment & feasibility

We review your land, establish your design criteria and discuss a realistic building budget, then sign a Preliminary Agreement.

03

Custom concept design

Our in-house Building Designer develops preliminary plans around your lifestyle, land, design ideas and budget.

04

Design review & refinement

Refine layout and elevations: room sizes, living spaces, outdoor areas, natural light and window placement.

05

Preliminary cost estimate

A clear, itemised preliminary cost estimate based on the plans, proposed finishes, building specification and known site requirements.

06

Talk to our team

Contact us by phone, email or visit our Cairns office to get started.
