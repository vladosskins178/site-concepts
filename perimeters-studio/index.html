Who we are

Here, everyone is a guest

Perimeters Studio has been in business since 1997 and is owned and operated by Tamara Lomenda-Camacho. When Tamara opened Perimeters Studio she envisioned a destination that was different than other salons.

From the gracious reception to the soothing natural lighting, to the deep leather chairs, Perimeters exudes an ambiance of spoiling their guests.

  • Luxurious scalp massages
  • Personalized hair design and colouring techniques
  • Manicures, pedicures, gel nails, waxing, makeup and skin care
