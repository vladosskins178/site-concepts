We love to cater

You bring the occasion. We’ll bring the masa.

Weddings, birthdays, events, coursed dinners and work lunches. Tacos come assembled and priced by the dozen, or buffet style where guests build their own.

For parties larger than 30 we recommend buffet style — enough for 3 tacos of each choice. We’ll help you pick the right amount and stay on budget, and we’re flexible if you have a creative idea.

  • Feed the squad — $59: 10 tacos, a heap of fresh chips, guac and salsa, 2 Mexican cervezas or Jarritos
  • Preorder the day before for pickup from 10 am; same-day orders during business hours — call (208) 518-1517

Good to know

Before you come by

Can I order for pickup or delivery?

Yes — both locations take online orders for pickup or delivery: Downtown Spokane · Hayden.

When is happy hour?

3–5 pm daily, with 20% off all food and drink. Tacos are also $1 off every Tuesday from 5 pm to close.

Do you serve brunch?

Yes, in Hayden — Saturday and Sunday, 11 am to 2 pm.

Which days are you closed?

Downtown Spokane is closed Sundays; Hayden is closed Mondays.

How do I book catering?

Email catering@cochinitotaqueria.com or see the catering menu (PDF). For parties over 30 we recommend buffet style.
