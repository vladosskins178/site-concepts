Our story

Born & raised at the Farmers’ Market

Our family has been serving up this infamous Wisconsin icon ever since the day our mother invented the recipe, after a baking disaster ended our batch of empanadas destined for the market that day.

That happy accident was the birth of the mighty Hot & Spicy Cheese Bread — the perfect buttery blend of red pepper, chives, provolone and monterey jack cheeses, and our secret sweet dough that melts in your mouth.

  • “Tear It & Share It!” is our best advice

FAQ

Good to know

Is your Syene Road location still open?

Our Syene Road location is no longer open to the public. We’ve moved our retail bakery to 1219 Regent Street.

Do you have parking available?

Yes — we offer limited parking spots at 1219 Regent St, which can be accessed through the alleyway behind our building. Street parking is also available in the surrounding area.

How spicy is the Hot & Spicy Cheese Bread?

Our signature loaf has a mild heat from crushed red pepper — more of a subtle warmth than a true kick. Most customers find it flavorful, not spicy.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes! You can purchase one at 1219 Regent Street, or call us at 608-230-6581 for an e-card that will be e-mailed to you.

Do you have gluten-free options available?

Not at this time — all of our products contain wheat.

Do your products contain nuts?

Our kitchen currently uses almonds, pecans, and walnuts (no peanuts). All items are produced in a facility where cross-contamination is possible.
