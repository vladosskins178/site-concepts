Questions

Before you bring it in

Do you work with my insurance company?

We work with all insurance companies to make the repair process as smooth and stress free as possible. Whether you need assistance submitting a photo estimate to your insurance company or need work on a damaged vehicle, we are here for you.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. No obligation: estimates are always free. Request a free estimate or call 1-859-317-8545.

How do I take good damage photos?

Take photos in daylight or bright, even lighting. Photograph all four corners of the vehicle, even undamaged areas, get close-up shots of every dent, scratch, or broken part, and include a photo of your license plate and VIN (dashboard sticker).

Can I submit photos through CCC ONE?

We use CCC ONE as our estimating system. If your insurance company uses CCC ONE, you may be able to submit your photos directly through their customer portal. Contact us and we will walk you through the process.
