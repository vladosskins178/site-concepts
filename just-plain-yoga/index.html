Good to know

Getting started is simple

I’ve never done yoga. Is that okay?

Yes. You do not need to be flexible, fit, spiritual, or experienced to begin. Our classes are approachable for all levels.

How do I book a class?

Find a class you like on the schedule, choose in-studio, virtual, or on-demand, and click Join to create your account.

Can I practice from home?

Yes. Join classes live by livestream, or practice anytime with our on-demand library.
