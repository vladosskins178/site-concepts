Good to know

Before your visit

Do I need a consultation before booking?

No, you don’t need a consultation for every service. Consultations are required for corrective colour and extensions.

What is your cancellation policy?

To reschedule or cancel, please contact the salon a minimum of 24 hours before your service. Within 24 hours, you may be charged the full price of all scheduled services.

What’s the eco fee?

For a small fee of $3.50 per visit, you’re contributing to planet-saving initiatives: responsibly disposing of salon waste, high-efficiency water and light fixtures, and carbon offset projects.

What should I do before my appointment?

Bring inspiration photos, arrive with clean, dry hair, and be prepared to discuss previous colour services and your hair history.
