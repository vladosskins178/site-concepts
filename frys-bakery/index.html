Pizza Sunday

Every Sunday, a pizza shop

Every Sunday, we turn the bakery into a pizza shop. The pizza team creates a new weekly menu using ingredients from local Vancouver Island farmers.

Our naturally leavened crust is crafted from freshly milled durum and spelt, finished in our wood-fired oven.

  • Limited bread, doughnuts and pastries are available at 9 am
  • Pizza starts at noon and goes until the dough runs out!

Since 1897

A family bakery, back across the street

Fry’s Bakery was started in Moosejaw, SK, in 1897 by Byron Fry’s great-great-grandfather, Charles Fry. In the 1920s, the Frys relocated to Vic West, where they opened their bakery at 403 Craigflower Rd.

In 2010, unaware of this family history, Byron built a wood-fired oven on a trailer and began baking bread for farmer’s markets. In 2012 Fry’s moved into the storefront at 416 Craigflower Rd, across the street from its historic location.
