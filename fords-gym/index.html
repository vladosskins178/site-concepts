Good to know

Before your first visit

Can I try the gym first?

Yes. A one-week free trial membership is available to non-members living in the Madison area. It is not available for tourists or visitors.

How much is a day pass?

A Daily Pass is $10. A punch card for 15 visits is $140.

Do you have showers?

Yes, locker & shower room facilities.

Is physical therapy available?

Yes. Via Physical Therapy, founded by Matt Ellison, DPT, now offers physical therapy at Ford’s Gym.
