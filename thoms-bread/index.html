Our breads

Flour, water, salt, and yeast

The heart of the bread relies only on flour, water, salt, and yeast, producing something which is beyond terrific.

Rustic Italian

Truly exemplifies the wonderful simplicity of a basic Italian loaf. It crackles as you bite through the crust and satisfies with a meaty crumb that is also light and chewy.

Focaccia

Traditional Roman-style focaccia. Hearty crumb that is also light and chewy. Buttery flavor rich in olive oil.

Ciabatta

Crusty on outside, with a chewy open interior. Great for sandwiches, panini, and dipping.

Cranberry Pecan

The perfect balance of sweet cranberry and buttery pecan, with a chewy center and hearty crust.

Chocolate Boule

Truly a decadent indulgence. Simply BREAD with Chocolate. Thom’s version of pain au chocolat.

Baguette

Long and crusty, Thom’s Baguettes are a True French offering. Great for sandwiches and crostini alike.
