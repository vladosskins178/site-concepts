Good to know

Before you book

Can I bring a cake?

Yes. You’re welcome to bring a cake, no fee will apply.

Is Amigos BYO?

We are fully licensed and not BYO.

Can I bring a pram?

Due to the nature of the building and the limited space, we can no longer guarantee that we can cater for prams. We are happy to provide high chairs and booster seats and store your pram for you; if you have tiny little ones, please bring a capsule just in case.

Are there surcharges?

1.5% fee applies to all credit card transactions. Weekend and public holiday surcharges apply.

How long is my table?

Reservations on Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday & Saturday have a limited time allocation.
