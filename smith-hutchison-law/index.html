Areas of practice

One firm for your business, your family, your property

We offer our individual clients a wide range of legal services including corporate, wills & estates, real estate conveyancing, employment and civil litigation.

01

Corporate, Commercial & Business Law

We can help you set up your new business, buy or sell, or restructure an old one, arrange for financing, or help you with any other aspect of your professional and business needs.

02

Wills and Estates

We can prepare both simple and complex wills, living wills, powers of attorney and trusts. We also probate estates, provide estate planning advice and handle contested estate matters and applications under the Wills Variation Act.

03

Real Estate

We act in the purchase, sale and leasing of private and commercial properties, homes and condominiums, including mortgages. We can also help developers obtain necessary permits for renovations and new construction projects.

04

Employment & Wrongful Dismissal

We advise both employers and employees on how to structure termination packages, lay offs and severance. If settlements cannot be reached, we work to protect our clients’ rights in Court and before Boards and Tribunals.

05

Civil Litigation

Breach of contract, professional malpractice, professional negligence, breach of privacy or some other tort: we provide advice and, where necessary, take legal action. Services at all court levels, provincial and federal.

06

Administrative Law

Smith Hutchison can help you deal with administrative tribunals such as the Workers Compensation Board, the Residential Tenancy Office, the Human Rights Tribunal and the Office of the Public Trustee.
