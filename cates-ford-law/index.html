Shaping our region for more than a century

Cates Ford Soll & Epp LLP was originally founded in 1908 by Ashcroft native, Fredrick Temple Cornwall.

Our experienced lawyers have represented clients at every level of court, from provincial to the Supreme Court of Canada, and have acted in some of British Columbia’s most important legal decisions and transactions.
