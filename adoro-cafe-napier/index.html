Welcome to Adoro

Great coffee, fresh food & friendly service

A favourite café for locals and visitors in the heart of Napier CBD, inside the historic Old Post Office Building on the corner of Dickens and Hastings Street.

Our French pastries are imported from France and freshly baked on the premises, our cakes are handcrafted by our experienced in-house patisserie chef, and our cabinet selection is freshly prepared by our talented kitchen team.

  • Coffee made with a special blend created exclusively for Adoro
  • T Leaf teas, matcha and refreshing cold drinks
  • Gluten-free, vegetarian, Dairy Free and Keto Friendly with Home made keto Bread
