Good to know

Before you come in

How much does it cost?

$6 Game Play Fee plus a minimum spend of $4 on food & drink: $10 per person (minimum) for up to 3 hours.

Can I make a reservation?

Reservations can be made most days of the week depending on availability. Reserve online. The form will not let you book within 20 minutes of arrival; if you are coming in shortly, please call to reserve.

Do you have a time limit?

We have a 3-hour max seating time. We will usually give you about a 30 minutes heads-up before we need the table. If a night is unexpectedly slow, we can extend your stay at the same hourly rate of $2/person per hour.

What about groups larger than eight?

For groups larger than eight (8) people, please contact us by phone or email to check availability and arrange your booking.

Are you licensed? Are kids allowed?

Yes, we have several draught beer options, wine, ciders, hard seltzers and other mixed drinks. And yes, we are a family-friendly environment with lots of great games for kids.

Where do I park?

You can park anywhere around or behind 321 Weber St N (the Brown Building we are located within). Avoid parking behind the Naval Association (Grey building south of us).
