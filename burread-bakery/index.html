Meet the baker

Great bread brings people together

Burread Bakery was founded by Michael Burr, whose journey began in the kitchen as a chef before discovering a true passion for baking. While living in Slovakia with his now wife, Monika, he found inspiration in the rich traditions of European bread-making.

The name Burread Bakery blends our family name, Burr, with our love for bread. It’s simple, personal, and a reflection of what we care about most: baking honest, high-quality goods for our community.

Sourdough pastries

Rare, technical, worth the wait

We offer a wide variety of pastries, including traditional and sourdough croissants, cruffins, tarts, and brioche. Our sourdough pastries are a true highlight: still quite rare and highly technical to produce.

Our pizza crust is made with organic flour and is slow-fermented to develop rich flavor and the perfect texture.
