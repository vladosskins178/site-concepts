Good to know

Before your visit

How do I book?

Book online through our booking page or call (423) 648-5353.

How much are extensions?

Extensions are priced by consultation only, with a 50% deposit required.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

We require more than 48 hours notice. Changes within 48 hours carry a 25% rescheduling fee, day-of cancellations 50%, and a no call / no show 100%. Fees are based on the $ amount of the scheduled services and are non-refundable.

What if I am running late?

If you arrive 15+ minutes past your appointment time we may ask you to reschedule, with a 15% rescheduling fee.
