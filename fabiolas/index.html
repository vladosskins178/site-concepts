Spaghetti Corners

A Greenbush tradition, picked back up

Fabiola’s Spaghetti House & Deli brings red sauce back to Madison’s historic Greenbush neighborhood. The Greenbush was once home to a tight-knit, diverse community of Italian and Jewish immigrants and African American families.

A stretch of Regent Street had so many Italian restaurants that the corner at Park was nicknamed “Spaghetti Corners.” Our menu picks up where those spaghetti houses left off.
