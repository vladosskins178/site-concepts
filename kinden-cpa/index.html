Online accounting firm FAQs

Questions we hear a lot

Do you use cloud-based accounting software?

Yes. At Kinden CPA, we are proud to be designated as a Quickbooks online accountant. Using cloud accounting tools allows us to help you more efficiently while passing the savings on to you.

What cities do you serve?

We are involved in all Canadian cities such as Halifax, Toronto, Mississauga, Vancouver, Edmonton, Montreal and Calgary. We can do bookkeeping and taxes, regardless of where you are situated in Canada, as we are 100% remote.

Why use Kinden CPA?

Kinden CPA is a cloud accounting firm. Hiring an online accountant leads to quicker communication and turn around time, and we offer a wide range of packages tailored to your needs and the current stage of your business.

In-house accounting or virtual?

For small businesses it becomes unrealistic to hire full-time accounting professionals in-house. A virtual accounting team will dramatically reduce your expenses and improve efficiency.
