Weddings and corporate events

Our catering services specialize in creating menus that work within your budget and your needs without compromising on quality. Whether it’s vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, or any other dietary need, we’ve got you covered.

With our buffet-style service, we handle setup, cleanup, and even offer delivery or pick-up options. For weddings, enjoy a complimentary tasting to ensure the perfect meal for your special day.

About us

Chef Mark Brunet, 28 years in the kitchen

Fresh Urban Plate embodies the culinary vision of Owner and Head Chef Mark Brunet. Mark’s expertise spans both Corporate and Private Chef roles, gracing the kitchens of prestigious venues in Toronto and catering to exclusive events across the U.S. and the Bahamas.

In 2013, Mark established Fresh Urban Plate, a bistro and catering company that originated in the Peterborough Wellness Centre and now flourishes at its lively lime green storefront on Water Street.
