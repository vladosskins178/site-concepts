FAQ’s

Your questions answered

Should I book my treatments?

Yes, we are by appointment only. Bookings can be made online, or by phone. If you are after specific time slots (after hours) we recommend booking at your earliest convenience.

How do I know which treatments are suitable for me?

We offer an in depth skin analysis that you can book online. A 15 minute skin analysis will help guide you onto your skin journey.

When I arrive, where do I go?

Craft Skin Co is located inside of Arch Cosmetic Tattooing on Alma Road, New Lambton, a few shops down from Corner Lane Espresso. Enter through the glass doors and head straight up the stairs.

Where do I park?

On street parking is readily available. Please respect our neighbours by not parking across their driveways, and allow a little extra time at school drop off and pick up.

Do you sell skincare online?

Yes. Shop carefully curated cosmeceutical skincare from our online store.
