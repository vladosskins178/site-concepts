Before your visit

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. New Patients Welcome. To schedule a New Patient appointment, call 519-576-7262 during office hours.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Walk-ins & Emergencies Welcome! Call 519-576-7262 and let us know what is going on.

Do you offer evening appointments?

Yes. We offer flexible scheduling, including evening appointments. On Thursday the office is open 10 am – 7 pm.

I get nervous at the dentist. Can you help?

Gentle dentistry is at the heart of our approach. Sedation dentistry options are available for patients with dental anxiety.
