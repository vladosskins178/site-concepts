Our inspiration

Inspired by Monica Brewster

Monica’s Eatery is an environment inspired by the life and spirit of Monica Brewster.

Highly respected by the local community, Monica was progressive for her time, innovative, creative and generous.

Good to know

Before you visit

Do I need to book?

For bookings of 10 or less just come on in! For a group of 10 or more please call us on (06) 759 2038 and leave a message.

Do you have gluten free options?

Our kitchen is not a gluten free environment as we prepare food items that contains gluten. While we do our absolute best to provide gluten free meals where listed, we cannot guarantee there may not be traces of gluten. Please talk to one of our team.

Is there a card surcharge?

Credit card and payWave transactions incur a 2% surcharge.
