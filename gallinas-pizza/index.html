The Gallina’s

Family owned and operated since 1978

Located in an historic area of downtown Springfield, Illinois is a restaurant known for its authentic Italian food and, relaxing atmosphere.

Gallina’s has been family owned and operated since 1978 and is dedicated to providing local residents and visitors to the area with a slice of Italy.

  • Authentic Italian food
  • Italian beers & wine: Peroni, Moretti and La Rossa when available
  • Friendly service, great prices
