About the studio

A truly boutique experience

By focusing on individual and small group training, we offer a truly boutique experience that allows you to focus on your success with a supportive and like-minded community.

All of our equipment is state-of-the-art from trusted brands, including Rogue and TRX. From barbells and conditioning equipment to yoga blocks and mobility tools, we meet you where you are.

  • Towels and a water bottle fill-up station
  • An assortment of health and beauty products to refresh after your workout
  • A welcoming, uncrowded environment

Getting in & parking

Use the entrance located off Queen St. to enter the lobby. Use the electronic keypad to search for Longevity Lancaster. The studio is down the ramp and is the first door on the right.

There is metered parking on Queen St., and the North Queen St. and Duke St. parking garages are within two blocks. Please do not use the lot adjacent to the Keppel Building.

FAQs

Good to know

How do I get started?

Schedule a consultation online, call or text (717) 440-8510, or email us. We respond to all inquiries within 48 hours.

What is a Certified Health Coach?

An active, nonjudgmental partner in helping you determine your lifestyle goals and your why, with training in nutrition, movement and exercise, stress management, and sleep hygiene.

Can I train with a friend?

Yes. Buddy personal training is a 60-minute session with you, a friend, and your personal trainer.

Do I need a membership to use the gym?

No. The Open Gym Day Pass gives you a full day of open gym access, no membership required.
