What's new

Complete implant treatment, in our office

Dental implants often are the best long-term options for tooth replacement, and we are very proud to offer complete treatment for dental implants under our roof.

With cone beam CT technology and our new digital scanner, we are able to plan our implants at a microscopic level, providing more predictable outcomes for our patients and shorter treatment times.

Common questions

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. Our receptionists will do their best to get you in as quickly as possible; most often on the same day you call. Call (204) 233-5164.

What if a tooth is knocked out?

Rinse it quickly with water, place the tooth in milk, and contact our office immediately. In situations when we can not be reached and significant swelling or trauma is observed, we recommend seeking emergency treatment at a hospital.

Do you see children?

Yes. We care for patients 6 months old to 99+ years old, with fluoride treatments, sealants and developmental monitoring.

Where do I park?

There is a free parking lot in rear, as well as convenient on-street parking right beside our front door.

What is your cancellation policy?

We request patients confirm their appointment. Appointments cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled time are considered failed appointments.

Do you handle insurance?

We strive to best manage all patient insurance/benefit plans and communicate the patient financial responsibility ahead of service dates. Major procedures require an approved pre-authorization from the insurance company.
