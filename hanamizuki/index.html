About Hanamizuki

An intimate dining experience, warm and relaxing

Experience the taste of Japan, but with a modern twist to this much-loved cuisine. Hanamizuki provides an intimate dining experience, with friendly staff, and a warm and relaxing atmosphere.

The entire restaurant can be reserved for groups of 50+ to ensure your special occasion is one to remember.

  • Vegetarian, vegan and gluten free options
  • Set menu available, perfect for groups to share
  • All meals can be shared if you wish to experience different varieties
