Our services

A full range of dental treatments

From simple to complex treatments, for the whole family.

01

Regular check-⁠ups & cleaning

Preventive care, fluoride treatments, fissure sealants and mouth guards for contact sport.

02

Gum treatment

Comprehensive treatments to both prevent and address gum disease with the aid of our registered dental hygienists.

03

Same day crowns

For most crowns, you can have the whole procedure done in one single day instead of two appointments.

04

Dental implants

Replace missing teeth without damaging the surrounding teeth. Ask Dr. Cardieri if dental implants are an option for you.

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening, plus composite or porcelain veneers, including Zoom light-accelerated in-office teeth whitening.

06

Root canals, dentures & extractions

Meet Dr. Liana Cardieri

Over 20 years of gentle dentistry

Dr. Cardieri is a graduate of the Federal University of Ceara, Brazil and has over 20 years experience as a family and cosmetic dentist. Since 2012 she is proudly serving her patients on her own practice in Kitchener, ON.

From simple to complex treatments, it is very common to hear from patients that she is very gentle and how painless the treatment was!

  • Six operatories, closed with glass walls and equipped with HEPA air purifiers
  • Intra-oral cameras and digital x-rays in all operatories
  • Dental hygienists Margaret Rapacz, RDH and Connie Roth, RDH
