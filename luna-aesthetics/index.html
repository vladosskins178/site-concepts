A new era of skin renewal

The Lunera Pixel Peel™

The Lunera Pixel Peel™ is Luna Aesthetics’ signature CO₂ resurfacing treatment, designed to renew, refine, and restore the skin at a cellular level. Powered by advanced fractional CO₂ laser technology from Alma, this treatment targets a wide range of skin concerns.

Designed with inclusivity in mind, this treatment is safe and effective for all skin types, including darker and melanated skin tones. Treatment intensity and recovery time are fully customizable.
