Good to know

Before your visit

Do you take walk-ins?

No, we don’t take walk-ins. We value our client’s time and want to ensure that we give them our full attention once they enter the salon. If you’re a new client, please request a consultation.

How long can the process take?

Full color transformations can take up to a full day, although most treatments don’t take that long! Quality takes time, and we don’t take shortcuts with your hair.

Do you provide services for men?

Yes we do. All genders are welcome and our pricing is gender neutral.

Do you do weddings or special events?

No, we don’t offer hair or beauty services for wedding parties or special events. As a boutique salon, we truly specialize in bespoke color and hair for our clients.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require at least 48 hours notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Late cancellations or no-shows will be charged the full service price. We do not charge this fee for those canceling due to illness.

I’m a returning client. How do I book?

Send a text message to 778-265-2009 or email info@staerkhair.com to book your next appointment with us.
