Our story

A cosy Italian inspired restaurant since 2004

We invite you to come in and experience our multi-award-winning restaurant, where our motto “mangia bene, mangia italiano”, eat well, eat Italian, exemplifies our approach towards great service.

From our humble beginnings in 2004 in the heart of New Plymouth, we relocated in 2011 into our current larger premises, with a more extensive range of both food and beverages.

  • Multi-award-winning restaurant
  • Italian and local beverages, including Italian beer Peroni
  • Most dishes can be made gluten free or dairy free
  • Designed for all types of groups and functions

Functions

Your special event, at Bella Vita

Ideal for weddings, birthdays, conferences, business dinners, Christmas parties and social functions. Choose from our canapé menu ($35pp) or a three-course set menu ($45, $54 or $64pp). Linen is provided complimentary for weddings.

Easy parking on the main street or in the Warehouse basement.
