Good to know

Before your first visit

Who can do the free 7-day trial?

You must live or work in the Madison area (please bring proof of residency) and not have had a trial or a membership within the last 3 years. The week trial is 7 days, used consecutively. Start your trial.

Is there an enrollment fee?

Memberships carry a $25 enrollment fee. No Enrollment Fee for Students, Teachers, Military & Healthcare Workers, available with in-person sign-up only.

How do I sign up for classes?

Download the MINDBODY app, or call us at 608-251-1245.

Where do I park?

Heated underground parking: $3 per visit from 7AM-7PM, or $2 with a Capital Fitness gift card. Free outside these hours. Call the front desk for the garage door code.

Questions or cancellations?

Email info@capitalfitness.net and expect a response during administrative hours, 8AM-6PM Monday-Friday.
