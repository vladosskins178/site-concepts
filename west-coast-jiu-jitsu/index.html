Jiu Jitsu programs

We have a spot for you

Our fundamentals program is fun and accessible to anyone, regardless of previous athletic experience. Whether you’re looking to get started or take your grappling to the highest levels.

Gi

A great place to start if you are looking to learn the basics. Our coaches lead you through foundational movements, techniques and goal-orientated live games.

No Gi

No gi training everyday! A mix of eco-style positional games and rolling, ideal for competitors and those looking for a high-paced, fun session.

Wrestling

A registered club under the BC Wrestling Association, home to Provincial and National medalists in freestyle wrestling.

Kid & Teens Jiu Jitsu

Empower your child with Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

At West Coast we teach respect, control, and self-discipline in a fun and social environment. We love seeing our young students gain self-confidence, build friendships, and improve their physical literacy.

We offer gi jiu jitsu for kids ages 5-8 three times a week. For kids and teens ages 9+ we have gi classes three times a week and no gi once a week. Our kids wrestling class is for all ages, all levels on Fridays.

  • Positional sparring and live game-style training
  • All levels welcome
  • A thriving kids competition team

Good to know

New to the mats?

Do I need experience?

No. Our fundamentals program is fun and accessible to anyone, regardless of previous athletic experience. We recommend our gi fundamentals class for beginners.

How do I try it?

Come try a free week today at 765 Broughton Street in Downtown Victoria (by the library). Get your free week trial.

What is included in a membership?

Gi, no gi, women’s only jiu jitsu, wrestling, and muay thai kickboxing classes, with access to our in-house weight training area.

Where do I park or leave my bike?

Our facility has a secure bike room and is beside a public parkade. There are showers too.
