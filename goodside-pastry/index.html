How to pre-order

Email us, we confirm

Send your order to info@goodsidepastryhouse.ca with your name, phone number, cake type, cake size and desired pickup date.

4” (serves 2-3) $21 · 6” (serves 6-8) $42 · 8” (serves 10-12) $72. Custom written chocolate plaques are an additional $3 (25 characters max).

  • Minimum of 48 hours notice for all cake preorders
  • Pastry pre-orders: by 4pm the day before pick-up
  • Your order is only confirmed once you receive an email confirmation

Established in 2020

Classics, with new-age twists

GoodSide was founded by pastry chefs Haley Landa & Curtis Helm, who each have over a decade of experience under their belts.

Having worked in and run many high-intensity kitchens, they have channeled their collective knowledge & skills into creating products that have elements of the classics & new age.
