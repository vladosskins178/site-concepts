Aftercare

Keep your lashes and tan looking fresh

How do I care for new lash extensions?

Avoid getting them wet for up to 24 hours after application (or ask for our Super Bonder at your appointment), do not rub your eyes, avoid oil based products, and clean with eyelash foam cleanser every 3 days.

How often do I need lash fills?

Lash fills are to be maintained every 2-4 weeks. Natural lashes grow out and shed daily, so regular fills keep your lashes looking full and undamaged.

When can I shower after a spray tan?

With the Studio Spray Tan, allow your tan to develop for 12-24 hours. With the Quickie Tan you can shower 4-6 hours after your appointment.
