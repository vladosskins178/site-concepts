About us

Treasured recipes from Uruguay

Empanada Gourmet offers a wide variety of Uruguayan baked goods: empanadas, masitas, alfajores, bizcochos, pasta frolla, fruit tarts, vegetable pies, and much more.

Our family descended from immigrants who colonized Uruguay and brought their own recipes. These recipes were considered “treasures”. We recreate them so you can experience a part of Uruguayan history through our delicate and unique baked goods.
