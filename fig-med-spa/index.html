Good to know

Before your first visit

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. Not sure where to begin? Book a complimentary consultation and our team will help you build a treatment plan tailored to your goals.

Who performs my treatment?

FIG is physician-led: every treatment protocol is designed and overseen by our medical director, Dr. Cristie Lehr-Hawkins, with a team of nurse practitioners, an aesthetic nurse specialist and licensed aestheticians.

How do memberships work?

All memberships are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Services do not roll over, and membership pricing applies to the member only and is non-transferable. Plans start at $99/mo.

How do I book?

Book online any time through our booking page, or call (918) 932-8810. Saturdays are by appointment.

Where do I park?

We’re in the Shops at South Yale, 8921 S Yale Ave, Suite B — free parking available.
