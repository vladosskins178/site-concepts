Services

Full service care, under one roof

Pet emergencies during clinic hours, elective surgeries, dentistry, x-ray and critical care. Vaccinations, parasite control and nutrition are all part of our preventive care approach.

01

Preventative care

Routine head-to-toe examinations with detailed estimates, tailored vaccination plans, flea prevention and worm prevention guidelines for puppies, kittens, indoor and outdoor pets.

02

Dental care

A full range of dentistry, dental x-ray and dental surgery at the clinic. All extractions are performed by our doctors and anesthesia is monitored by 2 people.

03

Surgeries & x-ray

Full surgical suite including x-ray and in house laboratory. Spays/neuters, elective procedures and laceration repair, with full general anesthesia and monitoring.

04

Behaviour consultations

Dr. Dudzic is pursuing specialist status certification in Behavior Medicine and is happy to assist any companion animal behavioral challenge, no matter how big or small.

05

Palliative care

An extra special area we continue to provide for our end of life patients. A collaborative close approach focusing on quality of life is key to ensuring a peaceful process.

06

Emergencies

We offer emergency pet care during clinic hours. After hours emergencies are directed to Central Victoria Veterinary Hospital at 250-475-2495.

Our approach

A slow, quiet visit, on your pet’s terms

We welcome new clients and cater to patients that have had trauma at veterinary and grooming facilities. Our goal is to perform only necessary procedures with the utmost consideration for your pet, tailored to their individual needs.

Safe pre-visit sedatives will be suggested if necessary. Call us if your pet has a particularly hard time with vet visits, has an anxiety concern, or is coming near the end of their journey with you.

  • Pre-visit medications for positive association over time
  • We listen to what you know is best for your family member

The team

Meet Dr. Eva Dudzic

Dr. Dudzic graduated from WCVM in Saskatoon in 1996. In 2001 she purchased Oak Bay Pet Clinic and expanded the practice to include dentistry; in 2015 it became a full service facility offering all surgical and radiology needs.

Her passions are medicine, geriatric and palliative care, and behavior medicine. We are a privately owned small team that considers every patient an integral family member.

Good to know

Common questions

What if my pet has an emergency after hours?

After hours emergencies are directed to Central Victoria Veterinary Hospital, 760 Roderick St. For immediate after hour response, call CVVH at 250-475-2495. During clinic hours, call us at 250-598-4595.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. We welcome new clients, including exotics like rabbits, guinea pigs, rodents and birds, alongside dogs and cats.

My pet is very anxious at the vet. Can you help?

Yes. Our focus is a slow, quiet approach, and safe pre-visit sedatives will be suggested if necessary. Call us for more details.

Do you do dental work on site?

Yes: a full range of dentistry, dental x-ray and dental surgery. All patients receive pre-anesthetic blood screening and IV fluids during each procedure.

Are you open on Saturday?

No, we are closed on Saturdays. Clinic hours: Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 10:30 AM - 6:30 PM, Wednesday 12 - 7 pm, Friday 1 - 6 pm.
