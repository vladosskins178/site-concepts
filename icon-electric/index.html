Meet the owner

Meet Tanner Lowe

To Icon Electric owner, Tanner Lowe, being an electrician came early in life. Tanner began his career in the electrical industry during high school as a laborer for a local Electrician.

After high school, Tanner enrolled in the Electrical Technology Program at MSU Northern. While working on his apprenticeship, Tanner graduated with honors and earned his AAS in Electrical Technology.

For the last several years, Tanner, along with his fiancee, Regan Lammers, have been working and planting their roots in the Billings, Montana area.
