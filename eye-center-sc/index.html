Our eye care specialties

A full spectrum of eye care services

Using the latest advancements in technology to provide the best outcome for our patients.

LASIK

Say "goodbye" to your glasses or contact lenses and experience the freedom of clear vision! Our state-of-the-art LASIK procedures can sharpen your eyesight with microscopic precision.

Cataract

Cataracts clouding your vision? Our skilled surgeons use advanced cataract removal techniques to provide clarity to your vision and a brighter outlook on the world.

Glaucoma

Three million Americans suffer from glaucoma, but only half know they have it. We specialize in early detection and management of glaucoma.

DURYSTA™

Do you struggle to get your daily glaucoma eyedrops in properly? Discover lasting relief from glaucoma with DURYSTA, designed to reduce intraocular pressure and preserve your vision.

Diabetic Eye Disease

Early detection can help prevent vision loss. Our experienced team specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of diabetic eye disease.

Posterior Capsular Haze

Blurry or hazy vision can occur following cataract surgery. A quick laser procedure can help.

Cataract surgery

Cataract surgery, safe, quick, effective

Cataract surgery is a safe, quick, and highly effective procedure designed to remove clouded lenses in the eye, restoring clear vision within one to three days.

Your procedure will take place in a state-of-the-art facility designed exclusively for eye surgery, giving you the best possible experience!
