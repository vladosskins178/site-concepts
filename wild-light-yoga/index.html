Your first class

Set yourself up for success

What should I bring?

Bring a mat, big towel and water bottle. We have filtered water at the studio so you can fill up your bottle. No mat? You can rent one at the studio for $3.

Do I need to be flexible?

No. Yoga is not simply a practice for the flexible; it requires just as much if not more strength as it does flexibility, and you will build both each time you practice.

How should I prepare?

Come in well hydrated, and eat 2-3 hours prior to class so you have some energy. Wear light workout clothing that allows for movement.

How do I sign up?

Sign up for classes online through our schedule or through the Mindbody app.

Are there discounts?

Full time students, veterans and 70+ get 20% off all pricing options except New Client Intro. Must be purchased in studio and cannot be combined with promotional discounts.

Can I practice yoga with an injury?

Generally, rest during the acute stage, consult with your doctor, and let your teachers know about injuries so we can assist you with modifications.
