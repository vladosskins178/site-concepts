FAQ

Good to know

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, we are accepting new patients. We are proud to serve Waterloo, Kitchener, and beyond.

What should I expect when coming in for the first time?

On your first visit, you will receive a complete dental check-up, including digital X-rays if necessary, and an extensive cleaning. Our staff will review your oral health goals and, if needed, develop a treatment plan.

Are emergency dental services available?

Yes, we offer emergency dental services, including toothaches, broken teeth, missing fillings, and other dental emergencies. Call 519-576-8510.

What payment options are available?

We accept most dental insurance plans, as well as cash, debit cards, and major credit cards. We accept the new Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), and financing is available with instant online approval.

Do you care for kids and families?

Absolutely! We provide complete dental services for the whole family, including children.
