Good to know

Before you call

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. We offer free consultations and flexible appointments for your convenience. Call (515) 953-0226.

Do you only work in Des Moines?

Our office is in Des Moines, and we help individuals throughout Iowa.

Can a juvenile get a jury trial or bail?

Two of the most significant differences from adult cases are that juveniles are not entitled to a trial by jury nor are they allowed to be released on bail.

Is bankruptcy my only option?

Not always. As bankruptcy lawyers, we consider whether our clients have other options to bankruptcy, based on a full and fair evaluation of your circumstances.
