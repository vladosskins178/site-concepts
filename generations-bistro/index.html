Our philosophy

Food as it was meant to be

What goes into every plate, and what never does.

Gluten-Free & Vegan Without Limits

Nearly every dish can be crafted gluten-free, vegan, or both — without cutting corners or sacrificing flavor. Strict cross-contamination standards for celiac safety.

No Seed Oils. Ever.

Our primary frying fat is grass-fed beef tallow. For vegan preparations we use organic coconut oil.

Ancient Grains & High Standards

Our wheat flour comes from ancient grains milled by Heartland Craft Grains in Lodi, WI. Our gluten-free bread is baked fresh by Sami’s Bakery in Florida.

Good to know

Before you visit

Can dishes be made gluten-free or vegan?

Yes. Nearly every dish can be crafted gluten-free, vegan, or both, with strict cross-contamination standards for celiac safety.

What do you cook with?

Grass-fed beef tallow is our primary frying fat, and organic coconut oil for vegan preparations. Our kitchen is free of seed oils.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, redeemable for dine-in, takeout, and everything in our marketplace. Purchase a gift card.

How do I reach you?
