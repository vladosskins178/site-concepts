About us

It all begins with you

Beauty Secrets is one of Hawke’s Bay’s leading day spas & beauty clinics, under continuous ownership in Ahuriri for 24 years. The purpose built clinic sits in the seaside shopping centre of Ahuriri.

We are not about spa trends, but offer spa treatments providing the utmost in relaxation and rejuvenation, along with the latest developments in massage and skincare, for both women and men.

  • All the team are internationally qualified therapists
  • Led by Rachel Jones, Owner & Senior Beauty Therapist
  • Environ, St Tropez, Lilash and LiBrow treatments and retail
