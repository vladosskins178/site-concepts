Before you visit

When are you open?

Tuesday through Saturday: lunch 11:00 am – 2:30 pm and dinner 5:00 pm – 8:30 pm. We’re closed Sunday and Monday.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. View the menu and order online ahead — no hassle.

Do you take reservations for large groups?

Yes. For large groups, please call us at (865) 247-6878 or email outhane@gmail.com.
