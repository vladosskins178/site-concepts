Adding spice to Adamstown strip

Family recipes, made to order

HaHa’s started during COVID as a pre-order takeaway pop-up from Merewether Uniting Church Hall on weekends, serving home-style Vietnamese dishes cooked by Maria and her mum.

The menu is a mix of Australian-style breakfasts with their own twist, along with Vietnamese street food options for lunch.

  • Signature vermicelli noodles with home made spring rolls
  • Bánh mì fresh made to order, all family recipes
  • Grilled seitan and tofu options for vegetarians
