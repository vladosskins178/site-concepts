Our menu

Fresh seafood, Australian beef & bush tucker

At Dundee’s on the Waterfront, our modern Australian menu is inspired by the flavours of Tropical North Queensland and crafted with the region’s finest local ingredients.

Every dish is made to order, using premium produce and house-made sauces. Finish with one of our indulgent desserts: rich, satisfying, and definitely worth saving room for.

  • Flame-grilled Australian beef
  • The freshest local seafood
  • Classic pasta dishes and seasonal salads
  • Native specialties like kangaroo and crocodile
  • Vegetarian, vegan, gluten free options available
