Flame-to-Table

Kebabs over an open charcoal flame

Flame-to-Table means that our kebabs are prepared using time-honored techniques that harness the power of an open charcoal flame.

This traditional method infuses our kebabs with a unique smoky flavor and enhances their tenderness.

  • Lamb, ribeye, Adana and tavuk kebabs

Connecting people with the flavors of Central Asia

Welcome to our Central Asian Restaurant! Nestled in the heart of Madison, we invite you to embark on a culinary journey through the vibrant flavors and rich cultural heritage of Central Asia.
